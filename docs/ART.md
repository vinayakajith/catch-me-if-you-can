# Character art

`apps/web/public/assets/balaji-expressions.png` was generated with the built-in image generation tool for this project. It depicts a fictional sample character, not a real uploaded person.

The atlas contains a three-column, two-row expression grid: normal, hiding, surprised, caught, crying, defeated. Runtime animation is performed by Phaser. Furniture is drawn from editable vector definitions so collision boundaries and foreground layers can match the art.

## Generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent sprite atlas for Father Hunter, a funny top-down Desi hidden-object game. Create one sheet with exactly six square cells in a strict 3-column, 2-row grid, evenly spaced, no drawn grid. Each cell shows the SAME fictional middle-aged Indian dad Balaji, a big round head with dark side-parted thinning hair, enormous thick black moustache, round rectangular glasses, warm brown skin, cream short-sleeved undershirt, small gold chain. Waist-up cartoon portrait, disproportionately enormous head, three-quarter front facing, consistently same size and framing in every cell. Expressive clean hand-drawn 2D cartoon, bold dark warm outlines, flat shaded warm ochre, coral, cream, teal accents; polished indie comedy game art, no realistic photographs. Cell order: top-left normal with smug suspicious eyebrows; top-middle hiding with eyes looking sideways and hands pulled to his chest; top-right surprised wide eyes and small open mouth; bottom-left caught with hands up in comical surrender; bottom-middle dramatic crying with two streams of huge teal tears; bottom-right defeated with drooping shoulders and sad eyebrows. All six portraits entirely within their equal cells with generous transparent margins. Isolated on genuinely transparent background. No text, no labels, no objects outside the cells, no watermark. Sheet aspect ratio 3:2, exact equal grid, suitable to split into frames by width/3 and height/2. Keep silhouette centered in each cell.
